Church Care Hub

A role-based church care application for visitor registration, member records, attendance, pastoral follow-up, and administrative oversight.

ReactTypeScriptViteSupabase
Source repository ↗Open live application ↗

Purpose and outcome

Organises visitor, member, attendance, and care workflows around the responsibilities of ushers, pastors, and administrators.

System architecture

React, TypeScript, and Vite provide the application interface. Supabase supports the data and authentication layer, with workflows organised by church role. Vercel hosts the deployed frontend.

Implemented capabilities

  • Usher workflows for visitors, attendance, and notes
  • Pastoral member records and care follow-up
  • Excel and CSV imports for pastoral users
  • Administrator oversight and role approvals

Typical workflow

  1. Sign in with an approved church role.
  2. Register a visitor or record attendance and visit notes.
  3. Use pastoral workflows to manage members and care follow-up.
  4. Review role approvals and system activity through administrator workflows.

Evidence and validation

The supplied public URL returned HTTP 200. Its sign-in screen was captured without signing in. Roles and capabilities were checked against the repository README; protected workflows and data permissions were not retested.

Limitations and operating context

Operational screens need an approved account and organisation configuration. This case study shows the public entry screen, not private member records.
